# Daily Plan & Habit Tracker

A practical, repeatable daily system focused on:
- Waking early
- Gym training
- Book reading
- Completing high-impact daily tasks

---

## 1) Core Daily Schedule (Weekday Template)

| Time | Activity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 5:45 AM | Wake up | No snooze, drink water, open curtains |
| 5:50–6:10 AM | Morning reset | Light stretch + 5 min breathing + quick plan review |
| 6:10–7:00 AM | Deep work block #1 | Most important task first |
| 7:00–7:45 AM | Gym session | Strength or cardio by plan |
| 7:45–8:15 AM | Cool down + breakfast | Protein-focused meal |
| 8:30 AM–12:00 PM | Work block #2 | Priority tasks, no distractions |
| 12:00–1:00 PM | Lunch + walk | 10–15 min walk after lunch |
| 1:00–4:30 PM | Work block #3 | Meetings + secondary tasks |
| 5:30–6:00 PM | Admin reset | Emails/messages/planning |
| 7:30–8:00 PM | Reading | 20–30 pages or 30 minutes |
| 8:00–9:15 PM | Wind-down | Prep next day, reduce screen use |
| 9:30 PM | Sleep | Target 8+ hours |

> If your day starts later, shift all blocks together by the same amount and keep sequence consistent.

---

## 2) Daily Non-Negotiables

Check these off every day:

- [ ] Wake up at planned time
- [ ] Gym or intentional movement (45+ min)
- [ ] Read for 30 minutes
- [ ] Complete Top 3 daily tasks
- [ ] Plan tomorrow before bed
- [ ] Sleep by target bedtime

## 4) Gym Plan (Simple 4-Day Split)

- **Mon – Upper Body Strength**
- **Tue – Lower Body Strength**
- **Thu – Upper Hypertrophy**
- **Sat – Lower + Conditioning**
- Wed/Fri/Sun: recovery walk, mobility, or light cardio

### Session Structure (45–60 min)
1. Warm-up (5–8 min)
2. Main lifts (20–25 min)
3. Accessories (15–20 min)
4. Cool down (5 min)

Goal: Add small progression weekly (reps, weight, or form quality).
